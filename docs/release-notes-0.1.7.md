# XiaoJia Platform 0.1.7

This source update includes manuscript quality gates and compatibility with the
configured DSH core. Startup preserves existing user model settings and never
rewrites provider protocols or substitutes a default model for missing credentials.

- Formal literature discovery and reading follow experiment/result validation and
  precede outlining and manuscript writing. Topic discovery retains preliminary
  evidence for the frozen protocol; later literature cannot silently retrofit it.
- Formatting checks re-verify final citations and recency independently. Missing
  coverage routes back to a distinct literature phase, not search or ghostwriting
  hidden inside citation verification. Classic foundational sources remain valid.

- Minor editorial findings no longer stop manuscript delivery solely because a
  reviewer labels the verdict `revise`. Their original wording remains recorded.
- Correctly disclosed source-access, author-declaration and journal-format items
  remain unresolved submission requirements, not automatic manuscript defects.
- Material scientific findings must bind a literal manuscript claim to supplied
  evidence, or identify a specific missing evidentiary obligation. Matching a
  quotation is not proof that the reviewer's interpretation is correct.
- An invalid or blocking review may receive one bounded adjudication on the
  existing independent review route. Both responses remain cached and auditable.
  An invalid adjudication stops the run; transport and balance errors do not
  initiate this fallback. No review history or revision allowance is reset.
- Minor and submission-only items do not initiate paid scientific rewriting.
- Reviewer output schemas explicitly include literal evidence excerpts; invented
  bracketed descriptions and paraphrases cannot stand in for source evidence.
- Correct repetition, table emphasis and accurate code-equivalence explanations
  are editorial issues, not automatic scientific contradictions.
- Hash-bound local archive manifests and software-test logs enter review context
  without certifying field performance or public-release permissions.
- Completed manuscript exports remain delivered while author-only requirements
  wait for input instead of marking the whole workflow as failed. An approval
  click rechecks unresolved requirements and cannot silently skip the gate.
- Complete imported bibliography entries are no longer formatted twice. A local
  layout helper preserves prose and native Word equation structure during QA.
- Project literature requirements remain blocking: at least 15 distinct research
  publications, at least 30% recent research, distributed support and substantive
  synthesis. Manuals and software documentation do not satisfy the research quota.
  Citation existence, identity, claim support and final binding are separate checks.
- Genuine invalid calculations, fabricated observations, hidden failed/null
  results, unbound citations and unsupported scientific claims remain blocking.

Manuscript completion does not establish field validation, source redistribution
permission, author approval, journal acceptance or submission readiness.
Startup patches support current component layouts and report skipped patches
without claiming success. Workbench consumers share one event subscription.
Older checkouts cannot overwrite a newer shared runtime. Restored research
presets and required academic skills are included in the repository.

Checkpoint stages cannot be advanced merely because export or audit files exist.
User data, credentials, local backups and one-off network repair scripts are
excluded from this source update. Desktop builds remain a separate release step.
